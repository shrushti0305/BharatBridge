Executive Summary

BharatBridge is a real-time multilingual event platform enabling instant live speech-to-text (STT), Gemini LLM context-aware neural translation, and sequential text-to-speech (TTS) synthesis across 22 official Indian languages + English.

1. Key Technical Features & Value Proposition

  • Context-Aware Natural LLM Translation: Powered by Google Gemini AI (Gemini 2.5/2.0 Flash) with conversational context-window prompts. Never translates word-for-word literally; preserves natural grammar, idioms, and conversational tone.
  • Strict Speaker vs. Listener Role Isolation: Speaker screen displays ONLY original spoken speech captions. Target translations and target audio TTS are suppressed for speakers to prevent confusion. Listeners receive real-time target translations and audio TTS.
  • Non-Blocking Sentence Queue & STT Deduplication: Web Speech API inputs pass through a FIFO queue (sentenceQueueRef) with a 2.5-second deduplication lock window to prevent line bleeding or duplicate STT invocations. Speech recognition runs continuously in parallel.
  • Zero-Overlapping Sequential TTS Audio Queue: Audio clips are managed by a single active player (activeAudioElRef). Incoming translated lines play strictly back-to-back in sequential order (ttsQueueRef) with zero voice overlapping.
  • Instant Guest Mode & Sub-100ms Catch-Up: 100% Guest Mode compatibility without mandatory logins. Newly joined listeners automatically receive the entire translated session history upon entering room PIN.
  • Complete Analytics & Export Options: Download transcripts as TXT, CSV (with UTF-8 BOM \uFEFF for native Excel Devanagari rendering), WAV audio recordings, and Gemini-generated AI Session Summaries.
